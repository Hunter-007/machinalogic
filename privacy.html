Privacy notice.

How Machina Logic handles personal information you share with us through this website.

Who we are

Machina Logic ("we", "us") is an operational technology security company based in Lekki, Lagos State, Nigeria. You can contact us about privacy at info@machina-logic.com.

What we collect

When you contact us, we collect the details you choose to provide, such as your name, role, organisation, email address, phone number, country and the content of your message.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering the fonts.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a briefing.
  • To prepare proposals and manage any engagement that follows.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Sharing and retention

We share your information only with people who need it to respond to you, and with service providers that support our business under appropriate confidentiality obligations. We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed for these purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you are, including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. To exercise these rights, email info@machina-logic.com.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
