Energy & utilities

Generation, transmission and distribution networks are expanding and digitising fast, with new substations, renewables and smart metering connecting to control centres. Every new link is a potential path to the grid.

Typical systems

  • SCADA / EMS
  • Distribution management
  • Substation automation
  • Protection relays
  • RTUs
  • Generation DCS
  • IEC 61850, DNP3, IEC 104

What's at stake

  • Outages affecting homes, hospitals and industry
  • Damage to high-value, long-lead equipment
  • Public safety and regulatory exposure

Where we focus

  • Control-centre and substation segmentation
  • Remote access to field assets
  • Monitoring of grid protocols

Oil, gas & mining

Remote sites, long equipment lifecycles, satellite and radio links, and heavy reliance on contractors make these operations hard to secure, and the safety consequences serious.

Typical systems

  • DCS
  • Safety instrumented systems
  • PLCs
  • Pipeline SCADA
  • Satellite / radio telemetry
  • Remote-operated equipment

What's at stake

  • Safety and environmental incidents
  • Lost production at hard-to-reach sites
  • Exposure through vendor and contractor access

Where we focus

  • Integrity and separation of safety systems
  • Securing remote and satellite links
  • Contractor and vendor access control

Manufacturing

Food and beverage, cement, FMCG, pharmaceuticals and more: production lines are increasingly linked to ERP and cloud analytics, and ransomware that starts in IT can stop the plant floor.

Typical systems

  • PLCs
  • HMIs
  • MES
  • Robotics & motion control
  • Historians
  • Legacy Windows workstations

What's at stake

  • Line stoppages and missed orders
  • Product quality and integrity
  • IT ransomware spreading into production

Where we focus

  • IT/OT segmentation and DMZ design
  • Protecting legacy systems that can't be patched
  • Backup and recovery of controller logic

Water & wastewater

Treatment works and pumping stations serve entire cities, often with small teams and tight budgets. Security here has to be practical, affordable and focused on public health.

Typical systems

  • SCADA
  • Treatment PLCs
  • Chemical dosing control
  • Pumping stations
  • Telemetry networks

What's at stake

  • Public health through dosing and treatment
  • Interrupted supply to communities
  • Limited staff and budget to respond

Where we focus

  • Remote pumping-station security
  • Integrity of dosing and treatment control
  • Budget-aware, high-impact controls

Ports & logistics

Africa's ports are investing heavily in automation. Terminal operating systems, cranes and gates now depend on networked control, and disruption ripples through regional supply chains.

Typical systems

  • Terminal operating systems
  • Crane control
  • Automated gates & OCR
  • Reefer monitoring
  • Building management

What's at stake

  • Cargo throughput and vessel schedules
  • Safety of heavy-equipment operations
  • Knock-on supply-chain disruption

Where we focus

  • Separating business systems from equipment control
  • Vendor remote access
  • Resilience and continuity planning
