Layer 01 / Discovery

Network cloaking

An asset that never answers can't be enumerated, scanned or exploited. BlastShield hides your OT assets from everyone except verified users, so an attack ends before reconnaissance is finished.

What it does

  • Hides PLCs, HMIs, RTUs and servers from scanning and AI-powered reconnaissance
  • Protects legacy devices that can't be patched, without touching their configuration
  • Shrinks your attack surface to only what you choose to expose

Why it matters in OT

  • Unpatched controllers stay in service for years. Cloaking protects them now, not after the next outage window
  • Automated exploit tooling can't target what it can't find
  • Hardware agnostic: no rip and replace of your existing network

Layer 02 / Access

Passwordless MFA & secure remote access

With no password, there's nothing to phish. Access is granted per identity to specific assets, never to the whole network. It replaces the shared VPN accounts that hand contractors far more than the job needs.

What it does

  • Passwordless multi-factor authentication for staff, contractors and vendors
  • Identity and device checks before any connection is allowed
  • Access scoped to named assets, not whole network segments
  • Secure remote access to SCADA, field sites and offshore platforms

Who it helps

  • Third-party vendors who need access for maintenance and repairs
  • Engineers supporting remote rigs, stations and substations
  • Security teams retiring shared VPN credentials

Layer 03 / Movement

Software-defined microsegmentation

Flat OT networks were built for reliability, not isolation. BlastShield limits traffic to authorised connections, so a compromise stays inside one segment. The blast radius stops at the boundary.

What it does

  • East-west isolation between zones and individual devices
  • Least-privilege communication: only authorised connections pass
  • Segmentation without re-cabling or network downtime
  • A practical way to put IEC 62443 zones and conduits in place

What you get

  • Lateral movement from a compromised workstation is contained
  • Granular visibility of traffic and access patterns
  • Continuous logging that simplifies audits and compliance evidence

Two out of three leaves a stage wide open.

Most OT prevention vendors deliver one of these capabilities. A handful deliver two. Cloaking without segmentation contains nothing once a valid credential is used. Segmentation without cloaking leaves every asset discoverable. Remote access without either protects the door and ignores the building.

BlastShield sits where all three overlap, delivered as one platform with one policy model.

BlastShield questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly

Do we need to replace our network hardware?

No. BlastShield is hardware agnostic and deploys over your existing network. There's no rip and replace.

Will deployment interrupt production?

BlastShield is designed to add segmentation without network downtime. We plan every phase with your operations and engineering teams and schedule changes around production.

Does our operational data pass through the cloud?

No. According to BlastWave, no data transits the cloud. Your OT traffic stays on your own network.

Can it protect systems we can't patch?

Yes. Network cloaking makes legacy and unpatchable devices invisible to anyone who isn't authorised, which removes them as targets while they stay in service.

We already use an OT visibility tool. Does BlastShield replace it?

No. Visibility and detection tools tell you what is on the network and what happened. BlastShield decides what can happen. The two work side by side.

Can we try it before we commit?

Yes. We can arrange a scoped trial on a representative site so your team sees the results on your own network first.

Who supports it day to day?
