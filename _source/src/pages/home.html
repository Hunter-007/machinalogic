The challenge

The infrastructure gap is a security gap.

Africa's industrial base is modernising fast. The security practice protecting it hasn't kept pace. Machina Logic exists to close that gap.

01

Convergence

Plants, grids and pipelines are being connected to corporate networks, cloud platforms and remote vendors, often faster than anyone can map what is now reachable from the outside.

02

Scarcity

OT security expertise is scarce everywhere, and scarcer still on the continent. IT teams are left defending controllers, protocols and processes they were never trained on.

03

Consequence

In OT, a security failure isn't just a data breach. It's a stopped line, a dark substation or an unsafe process, with physical, financial and public consequences.

Why OT is different

IT security playbooks break on the plant floor.

Operational technology runs physical processes, so the priorities, constraints and failure modes are fundamentally different from corporate IT.

Corporate ITOperational technology
First priorityConfidentiality of dataSafety and availability of the process
PatchingDays to weeks, often automatedMonths to years, tied to planned outages and vendor approval
Asset lifespan3 to 5 years15 to 30 years, often on legacy operating systems
Impact of failureData loss, business disruptionPhysical damage, safety incidents, environmental harm, public outages
