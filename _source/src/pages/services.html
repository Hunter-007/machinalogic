Asset visibility & architecture review

You can't defend what you can't see. We build a complete, validated inventory of your OT environment and map how it actually communicates, not how the drawings say it does.

What's involved

  • Passive traffic capture at key points, with no disruptive scanning of fragile devices
  • Review of drawings, configurations and firewall rules
  • Site walkdowns with operations and engineering
  • Mapping of communication paths and remote access routes

What you receive

  • Validated asset inventory: make, model, firmware and role
  • As-is network architecture diagrams
  • Register of remote and external connectivity
  • Prioritised list of architectural gaps

Threat detection & monitoring

Continuous monitoring built around industrial protocols and your process, so real threats stand out from normal plant behaviour instead of drowning in noise.

What's involved

  • Design and deployment of OT network monitoring sensors
  • Baselining of normal communications and process behaviour
  • Detection tuning for industrial protocols
  • Escalation playbooks agreed with operations

What you receive

  • Monitoring architecture and sensor placement plan
  • Tuned detection baseline for your environment
  • Escalation and response runbooks
  • Regular threat and activity reporting

Protocols we monitor

  • Modbus/TCP
  • DNP3
  • IEC 60870-5-104
  • IEC 61850
  • OPC UA
  • PROFINET
  • EtherNet/IP
  • S7comm
  • BACnet

Incident response

When something goes wrong, you need responders who know that containment in a plant can't simply mean "pull the plug." We work alongside your operators to protect people and process first.

What's involved

  • Rapid scoping of safety and production impact with operations
  • Evidence collection across OT and IT systems
  • Containment options assessed against process safety
  • Recovery planning with engineering and vendors

What you receive

  • Incident timeline and root-cause analysis
  • Containment and recovery plan
  • Executive and regulator-ready reporting
  • Lessons learned and hardening recommendations

Risk & vulnerability assessment

An independent view of how exposed your operations really are, ranked by what could physically happen rather than by generic IT severity scores.

What's involved

  • Assessment structured around IEC 62443 and NIST SP 800-82
  • Vulnerability analysis using safe, OT-appropriate techniques
  • Consequence-based scenario workshops with your engineers
  • Review of policies, procedures and third-party access

What you receive

  • Risk register prioritised by operational consequence
  • Findings mapped to recognised frameworks
  • Pragmatic, phased remediation roadmap
  • Board-level summary of risk and priorities

Compliance & regulatory advisory

Operating across several African jurisdictions means overlapping national, sector and customer requirements. We help you meet them in a way that works on the plant floor.

What's involved

  • Mapping of applicable national, sector and contractual requirements
  • Gap analysis against those requirements
  • OT security policy, procedure and governance development
  • Preparation for audits and regulator engagement

What you receive

  • Obligations register by jurisdiction and site
  • Gap analysis and remediation plan
  • OT security policy and procedure set
  • Audit-ready evidence pack

Questions we hear most.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly

Will your work disrupt our operations?

Our default is passive, non-intrusive techniques. Any activity that touches live systems is planned and agreed in advance with your operations and engineering teams, and scheduled around your production requirements.

Do we need to buy a particular product?

No. Our advice is based on your environment, risks and budget. Where tooling is needed, we'll explain the options and recommend only what fits.

We already have an IT security team. Why do we need OT specialists?

IT security skills are valuable, but industrial environments use different protocols, tolerate far less disruption and carry physical consequences. We work alongside your IT team to extend what they do into OT safely, and to build their confidence in doing so.

Which countries do you work in?

We're headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria, and support organisations with operations across Africa. Tell us where your sites are and we'll discuss how we'd deliver the work.

How does an engagement start?

With a confidential briefing call to understand your environment and concerns. From there we agree scope, access and safety considerations, and send a written proposal before any work begins.

How do you handle confidential information about our sites?

We're happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before detailed discussions. Information about your environment is shared only with the people working on your engagement and handled according to agreed controls.
