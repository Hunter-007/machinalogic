Who we are

Where industrial engineering meets cybersecurity.

Machina Logic is an operational technology security practice focused on the operators that keep African economies running: energy and utilities, oil, gas and mining, manufacturing, water, ports and the public agencies that oversee them.

We understand not just networks and endpoints, but control loops, safety systems and the operational cost of getting a fix wrong. Our work starts and ends with operational technology. It is not an IT product line adapted after the fact.

That focus shapes everything: how we scope engagements, how we test, how we respond and how we report. The question behind every recommendation is the same: will this make the operation safer and more resilient, without putting it at risk?

Why now

Industrial systems are now a front line.

Around the world, attacks on industrial environments have moved from theory to reality. The 2015 and 2016 attacks on Ukraine's power grid showed that control systems can be deliberately manipulated to cut power. In 2021, a ransomware attack on corporate IT systems led Colonial Pipeline to halt pipeline operations in the United States as a precaution.

Across Africa, infrastructure is being built and modernised at pace: new generation capacity, automated mines, smart water networks and highly automated port terminals. Each of these projects adds connectivity, and with it, exposure.

The opportunity is to build security in now, while systems are being designed and commissioned, rather than retrofitting it after an incident. That is the work Machina Logic was founded to do.
